Shadow Link’s visual identity combines a linked **S** circuit symbol with a high-signal green palette. The mark represents the connection between an operator’s past reactions and the response predicted in the present.

## Assets

| Asset | Intended use |
|---|---|
| [`shadow-link-wordmark.png`](assets/shadow-link-wordmark.png) | Repository headers, presentations, and wide layouts |
| [`shadow-link-symbol.png`](assets/shadow-link-symbol.png) | Avatars, app icons, and square layouts |

Both supplied assets include their near-black background. Preserve the artwork’s aspect ratio and leave clear space around the mark. Do not recolor, distort, rotate, or place it on a competing background.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Signal green | Primary mark, actions, terminal emphasis | `#5FF26D` |
| Deep green | Structure, borders, secondary details | `#1E3A2B` |
| Near black | Primary background | `#080D11` |
| Soft white | Wordmark and primary text | `#F4F7F8` |
| Muted gray | Secondary text | `#A9BBB5` |

The terminal UI uses these same roles, with amber reserved for warnings and red reserved for errors.

## Voice

**Name:** Shadow Link  
**Tagline:** The shadow of you.  
**Descriptor:** The local, terminal-native cognitive mirror.

Use direct, compact language. Product copy should emphasize local operation, learned behavioral patterns, and operator control without claiming that predictions are guaranteed.
